Bá Khí is a self-paced study site, not an exam platform.

Never add or keep: mock exams, timed tests, countdown clocks, time limits, auto-submit, leaderboards ranked by exam score. Any feature must encourage self-paced learning.

If an old page is found to contain a timer or exam mechanic, **report it first** — do not delete or rewrite it without explicit permission, because those pages are also protected by the content-integrity baseline.
